How the Wall Chooses

The controller's decision, in three drawings: how a minute's choice is assembled, the map that turns it into a clip name, and the loop that trains the beliefs.

The honesty rules

  • CONFIRMEvery fire is checked: the OSC send, then a REST read within 2 s. Only a confirmed fire advances the wall's recorded position or opens a reward window.
  • HOLDNo confirmation: the position stands, nothing is earned, and the next minute routes again from the truth.
  • BLINDThe crowd stream silent for 30 s: the controller stops trusting the room and walks the wall down its arm toward Barren until the data returns.
  • MIDNIGHTThe personality changes, the beliefs reset to its priors, the action lean and any pending reward are cleared.
