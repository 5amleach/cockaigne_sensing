# 2026-10-16 — first live test, studio shed, one real camera

One Reolink RLC-810A (cam1) on the closed PoE network, sub-stream, Sam in the
shed. First time the detector, the calibration and the full chain ran against
a live camera rather than a file.

## Stream and detector

- Sub-stream `h264Preview_01_sub` opened first try: **640×360**. One benign
  H.264 decode error over the whole session.
- GPU confirmed: torch 2.11.0+cu128 on an RTX 5070 Ti, CUDA available.
  ultralytics 8.4.178; yolo11s.pt downloaded on first use. Ultralytics
  auto-installed `lap` for ByteTrack on the first tracking run.
- The camera's on-screen overlays (Reolink logo, timestamp, camera name) are
  still on, and its clock is a day behind (showed 15/10). Both to be fixed in
  the camera's web interface; the overlays sit inside the frames the detector
  sees (logged 19 Sept 2026).

## bench_detect, 150 s live (`--seconds` added for this)

Sam walked the shed, stood still, sat, left. 691 frames in 150 s
(**0.22 s per frame** at the 5 fps pacing; model=yolo11s.pt, imgsz=1280,
device=auto resolving to the GPU).

Track ids: 5 distinct over the run.

```
id  1: 6.5 s   (22 frames)   walking in near the camera
id  7: 43.2 s  (195 frames)  the long walk + standing still — held throughout
id  9: 2.5 s   (14 frames)   sitting down / partly hidden
id 11: 6.9 s   (36 frames)   walk to the far end — detected right at the glass door, conf 0.88
id 14: 3.6 s   (19 frames)   last movements before leaving
```

Boxes sit tightly on the person in every inspected snapshot, standing and
walking, near and far; no boxes on furniture. The far end of the room is not
missed at 640 wide, so the main stream was not needed. The final ~55 s of
zeros were real: Sam had left the shed. Id churn happened at the hard moments
(entering at the frame edge, sitting into the egg chair whose high back faces
the camera); one id per person held through the ordinary walking and standing.
A person fully seated in that chair is invisible to this camera — worth
remembering when reading shed numbers, though no such chair exists at the venue.

## Calibration (`calibrate_auto`, far-wall-centre 320 153 confirmed by Sam)

- Fit: camera at x=1.75 m, **height 2.68 m, pitch 36.5° down**, 278 usable
  boxes, median box error 11 px. Sam confirms height and pitch roughly match
  the real mount.
- Sanity check: every bench box's feet map inside the floor
  (x 0.46–3.37 m, y 3.96–6.55 m, all within the 3.5 × 7.0 m rectangle).
- Written to `config/homography_cam1.json` (committed).

## Full chain + watch_bus, Sam on the viewing spot ~3 min

`cockaigne_sensing.run --config config/studio.yaml` with the bus on
ws://127.0.0.1:8765. One camera-lost/back pair during model warm-up, then
cam1 healthy for the whole session (age 0.0–0.2 s).

With the shed empty: n=0, ring 0, everything 0.00 — an empty room reports
itself. With Sam standing on the viewing spot:

- **n=1** held continuously, C (cohesion_relational) = 1.00 as specified for
  one person.
- cohesion_smooth climbed 0.03 → **0.72** and the reservoir followed to
  **0.72**; **ring stepped 0 → 1 → 2 → 3** over about a minute and held at 3.
- Ring 4 was not reached: the one-person score ceiling is 0.72 and ring_down[3]
  is 0.74, so a lone viewer tops out at Ring 3 by design. Observed exactly.
- Moods wobbled between happy / sad / bored with the dwell timer pacing the
  changes — the labels are provisional (logged 5 Oct 2026) and behaved as
  expected for a still person; nothing alarming.
- One brief dip (smooth 0.72 → 0.59 → recovered) mid-stand, likely Sam
  shifting; the reservoir barely moved, which is what it is for.

## Verdict

Detection, tracking, auto-calibration and the full chain all work on the real
camera. Good per the brief: the count matches the room, one id per person
holds through normal movement, boxes on people not furniture, and the ring
climbs and holds under a lone viewer exactly as the config says it should.
To do: switch off the camera's overlays and fix its clock; marker calibration
